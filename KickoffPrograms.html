Our Programs

Training built around where a child actually is.

Structured soccer programs for ages 5–14 in Benicia and the greater Suisun City area, grouped by age and skill level so every session fits.

Program Overview

One program, three developmental stages.

We provide structured, soccer-based training programs for children aged 5–14 in the Benicia and Suisun City area, designed to support both beginner and developing players.

Our program is especially welcoming to younger children who are new to sports or may be hesitant to start, offering a positive and low-pressure environment that focuses on building confidence, coordination, and comfort with physical activity. We prioritize fun, movement, and gradual skill development — both life skills and athletic skills — to help each child feel successful and engaged from their very first session. At the same time, more competitive players are able to receive a healthy, competitive environment oriented around development and direction for more serious athletic goals, so no player outgrows the program too quickly.

To make that balance work, players are grouped by age and skill level — 5–8, 9–11, and 12–14 — ensuring appropriate development and personalized attention at every stage. Sessions are led by collegiate-level athletes and supported by experienced childcare professionals, creating a unique balance between high-quality training and a safe, well-structured environment. Through a mix of fundamental skill work, movement-based activities, and small-sided games, we help children develop not only as soccer players, but also socially and athletically — building teamwork, communication, discipline, and a lasting interest in staying active.
