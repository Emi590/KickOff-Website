Our Mission

Building confidence before we build competitors.

We're a Benicia-based soccer training program committed to supporting our local football community while welcoming new players into the game.

We work with children ages 5–14 — from complete beginners to developing players — focusing on building confidence, developing fundamental skills, and bringing out each child's natural ability in a fun, active environment.

Sessions are led by collegiate-level athletes and supported by experienced childcare professionals, ensuring a safe, structured, and engaging experience for every family.

Confidence

A low-pressure space for kids to grow into the game.

Skill

Fundamentals taught with patience and purpose.

A connective culture around a rapidly growing sport.

Discipline, teamwork, and self-confidence, on and off the ball.

Programs

Grouped by age, coached to fit.

Players are grouped by age and skill level so every session matches where a child actually is — not just how old they are.

5–8
Group 01

First Touches

An especially welcoming entry point for kids who are new to sports or hesitant to start — built around movement, coordination, and comfort.

  • Confidence-first, low-pressure environment
  • Coordination & movement fundamentals
  • Introduction to teamwork and following structure
9–11
Group 02

Building Blocks

Fundamental skill work paired with small-sided games, helping developing players sharpen technique while having fun with their peers.

  • Technical skill development
  • Small-sided games & game IQ
  • Communication and discipline
12–14
Group 03

Next Level

A healthy competitive environment for more serious athletes, with direction from collegiate players pursuing further athletic goals.

  • Competitive, development-oriented training
  • Direct mentorship from college athletes
  • Pathways toward competitive play
Competitive Advantage

Local roots. College-level coaching.

Our advantage is built on trust — the kind that comes from being part of the community we coach in.

01

Deep Local Network

Strong connections across Benicia, Fairfield, and Vallejo give us direct access to families, players, and youth soccer networks — plus trusted relationships with local club leadership.

02

Firsthand Credibility

Built through hands-on experience as a referee in the local scene, giving us early trust and adoption within the community from day one.

03

The Gap We Fill

We focus on the beginner-to-development pathway — structured training for kids new to the game, while also supporting those transitioning into competitive play.

04

Personal, Not Corporate

Unlike larger clubs, we offer personalized attention and a community-centered approach — with the operational flexibility to grow into new areas without losing quality.
