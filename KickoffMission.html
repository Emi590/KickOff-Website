Our Mission

Confidence first. Skills second. Trophies eventually.

Why KickOff exists, who it's built for, and what we're trying to grow in this community — one session at a time.

The Full Story

A Benicia-based program built around people, not just players.

We are a Benicia-based soccer training program committed to supporting our local football community while welcoming new players into the game. We work with children ages 5–14, from complete beginners to developing players, focusing on building confidence, developing fundamental skills, and bringing out each child's natural ability — all inside a fun, active environment designed to feel approachable rather than intimidating. Whether a child has never touched a ball or is already dreaming about playing competitively, our sessions meet them exactly where they are and give them a reason to want to come back.

Our sessions are led by collegiate-level athletes and supported by experienced childcare professionals, which means every practice is both technically sound and genuinely safe. That combination lets us serve families by creating a positive space where kids can grow, stay active, and develop a lasting love for the game while building discipline, teamwork, and self-confidence — skills that carry well beyond the field. As youth soccer continues to grow rapidly across our region, we hope KickOff can contribute to that connective culture, giving more local families an accessible, well-run entry point into the sport.

Confidence

A low-pressure space for kids to grow into the game at their own pace.

Skill

Fundamentals taught with patience, structure, and purpose.

Community

A connective culture around a rapidly growing sport in Benicia and beyond.

Character

Discipline, teamwork, and self-confidence, on and off the ball.
